immutable user-experience archive. All dates are review dates, not validity
or flight-schedule guarantees. Previous source audit:

## Evidence not available

The newly announced `www.iranair.ir` and `ebooking.iranair.ir` were not
retrievable through the public web reader during this audit, as in the
previous audit. This does **not** demonstrate global site downtime.
Neither its current rule tables nor the live booking/check-in experience
could be inspected. For this reason, numeric current baggage restrictions,
published tariff codes, specific cancellation amounts, current online
check-in acceptance, seating plans, inflight Wi-Fi and service eligibility
are not asserted. The dedicated inflight-connectivity section remains draft.
Online sources also give incompatible numbers for the size and state of
Iran Air's fleet; keep all family counts null.
