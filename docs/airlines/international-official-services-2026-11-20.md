## Details and publication constraints

- **Pets**: distinguish cabin vs hold eligibility, country restrictions and advance booking. Qatar Airways does not offer ordinary in-cabin cats/dogs as a general service; narrow exceptions for falcons and eligible service dogs are described accurately.
- **Batteries**: links to official restricted-items guidance, with no arbitrary universal capacity limit. Carry-on-only limitations are explained where the company specifies them.
- **Fleet**: two sourced model-family snapshots per airline, **count=null**, **configuration_fa=null**, `status=unknown`, `relationship=unknown`. A family listing is not proof of today's active fleet size, ownership or a particular service.
- **Cabin / lounges**: source-backed general descriptions, no claim that Qsuite, premium seating or lounge eligibility applies universally. Pegasus cabin/lounges remain drafts pending distinct product/eligibility validation.
- **Onboard connectivity**: differs by aircraft and fare. Pegasus Fly & Watch uses local in-cabin Wi-Fi for entertainment, **not guaranteed general internet access**.
- **Network**: remains a draft for all seven, pending dated carrier-specific route/operating-carrier evidence rather than manufactured schedules.

The 51 archived traveler experiences and their original author names are not modified. Existing airline `experience_links` stay pending with no consent inference; `published_reviews` stays empty and no synthetic star scores are created.

Android and Cloudflare readers use the same published-section metadata. App online refresh picks up changes from `main`; offline fallback requires a later manual APK build. No APK workflow triggers are changed.
