### Important qualifications
- **SRY**: August 2026 reports document Dasht-e-Naz runway rehabilitation with suspended / relocated flights; October reporting indicated work was ongoing. There is **no verified November reopening**. Never claim flights are now operating from SRY solely from the identity index.
- **BUZ**: reports describe current terminal redevelopment needs, not a completed new terminal; a previous official public-relations notice describes eastern car-park limitations without confirmed expiry.
- **ABD**: May 2026 domestic flight operational announcement is historical, not a current timetable or proof of international services.
- **AZD**: a local municipal transport report supports an airport taxi stand; no current fare or confirmed hours are quoted.
- **RAS**: airport development plans are not published as finished passenger services.
