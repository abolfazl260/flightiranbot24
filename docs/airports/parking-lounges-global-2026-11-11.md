## Doha — DOH

### Parking
- Mawaqif Qatar, the parking operator page identifying its HIA service:
  https://www.mawaqifqatar.com/booking/site/hia/
- The operator lists three sections (East, West and Car Park C), **3,325
  spaces in total**, and approximately **34 accessible spaces**. East is
  described as more convenient for Qatar Airways passengers; part of West
  is reserved for authorized government use.
- An HIA operational FAQ update reported by The Peninsula in April 2026
  stated that valet services were then unavailable:
  https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/22/04/2026/hia-updates-faqs-on-airline-partners-airport-operations-in-qatar
  The guide therefore does not promise valet availability. The Mawaqif
  portal remains the source for current availability, rates and bookings.

### Lounges
- Official airport maps:
  https://dohahamadairport.com/sites/default/files/maps/pdf/Duty%20Free%20South%20Map%20-%20August%202024%20update.pdf
  https://dohahamadairport.com/sites/default/files/maps/pdf/Duty%20Free%20North%20and%20Garden%20Map%20-%20August%202024%20update_0.pdf
- Qatar Airways official lounge directory:
  https://www.qatarairways.com/en-tw/lounges.html
- Discover Qatar Al Maha booking:
  https://www.discoverqatar.qa/departure-al-maha-lounge/overview
- Maps identify Al Safwa First, Al Mourjan Business, Al Maha, Oryx,
  Platinum & Gold, and Silver lounge products. Qatar Airways describes
  Al Safwa, Al Mourjan and Privilege Club lounges. Discover Qatar lists
  paid Al Maha access up to six hours.
- Airport maps found in this review are dated 2024; the guide instructs
  passengers to confirm exact location and current access before entry.

## Dubai — DXB

### Parking
- Dubai Airports official parking and terminal fees:
  https://dubaiairports.ae/transport/car-parking
- Official parking FAQs:
  https://dubaiairports.ae/faqs
- The guide records Premium and Economy options and walking distances
  by terminal: T1 Premium 2–3 minutes / Economy 7–9 minutes; T2 Premium
  3–5 minutes / Economy 10–12 minutes; T3 approximately 2–3 minutes.
  It also explains QR-ticket payment, automatic payment machines and
  Salik where applicable.
- DXB does not provide a separate Arrivals pick-up area; the airport FAQ
  directs pick-ups to the terminal car parks. Rates are not hard-coded
  because they vary by terminal, zone and duration.

### Lounges
- Official Dubai Airports Relax & Refresh directory:
  https://dubaiairports.ae/experiences/relax-refresh
- The airport directory includes Ahlan, Plaza Premium, British Airways,
  Emirates and other lounge products across T1, T2 and T3. It also lists
  hotels, spas, family areas and an Assisted Travel Lounge. The guide
  explicitly distinguishes these different facility types and does not
  imply every lounge is available to every passenger.

## Frankfurt — FRA

### Parking
- Official driving and parking information:
  https://www.frankfurt-airport.com/en/transport-and-parking/to-from-the-airport/travel-by-car.html
- Official parking products and online booking:
  https://www.frankfurt-airport.com/en/transport-and-parking/parking-offers.html
- Fraport lists terminal, business, holiday, eParking and accessible
  parking products. The guide notes that T1 arrivals parking is currently
  limited due to construction and directs pick-ups to the multi-storey
  car park. The 10-minute curbside allowance is described with its
  two-per-calendar-day limit and the three-hour maximum.
- Prices and construction routing are volatile and are delegated to
  the official live pages.

### Lounges
- Official lounge directory:
  https://www.frankfurt-airport.com/en/at-the-airport/facilities-services/lounges.html
- Examples include the Air France/KLM lounge in T3 Pier H (Schengen),
  Luxx Lounge in T1 public area, Airport Club Frankfurt, Lufthansa
  Business/First Class lounges in T1, Air Canada Lounge and Priority
  Lounge in T3. The guide tells users to verify public/security-side
  location, terminal and live opening status.

## Kuala Lumpur — KUL

### Parking
- Malaysia Airports official T1 parking:
  https://airports.malaysiaairports.com.my/transit/klia1/parking
- Malaysia Airports official T2 parking:
  https://airports.malaysiaairports.com.my/index.php/klia2/air-travel/parking
- T1: more than 5,700 terminal-car-park bays, over 5,200 LTCP bays and
  more than 300 Preferred Parking bays. Preferred Parking is about a
  two-minute walk, terminal parking about three minutes; LTCP shuttles
  run every ten minutes and connect to T1/T2.
- T2: the eight-storey gateway@klia2 car park is directly connected to
  the main terminal through the mall; security staff operate 24 hours.
  Malaysia Airports also lists LTCP as an alternative for T2 passengers.
- The guide directs users to the live terminal-specific rate and booking
  pages rather than duplicating volatile rate tables.

### Lounges
- Malaysia Airports T1 activities and common-use lounge guide:
  https://airports.malaysiaairports.com.my/flight-information/klia1/booking-services/things-to-do
- Malaysia Airports T2 facilities:
  https://airports.malaysiaairports.com.my/node/klia2/air-travel/facilities
- T1: common-use lounges and Sama-Sama Express are in the Satellite
  Building; Airport FastTrack Lounge is on Level 5 of the Main Terminal.
  Malaysia Airports notes that FastTrack users must clear immigration
  and customs.
- T2: the official facilities list Sama-Sama Express, shower facilities
  and a TV Lounge (movie/sports). The guide does not mislabel the TV
  lounge or transit hotel as an airline business lounge.

## London Heathrow — LHR

### Parking
- Official Heathrow parking catalogue:
  https://www.heathrow.com/transport-and-directions/heathrow-parking
- Park & Ride details:
  https://www.heathrow.com/transport-and-directions/heathrow-parking/heathrow-park-and-ride
- The guide distinguishes Terminal Parking, Park & Ride, Priority Park
  & Ride, Meet & Greet, Valet and T5 Pod Parking.
- **2026 operational changes:** Terminal 4's multi-storey Terminal
  Parking closed on 23 June 2026; passenger parking for T4 moved to
  Zone A of T4 Park & Ride. Priority Park & Ride for T2/T3 is currently
  unavailable for new bookings and drive-up stays; T5 remains open.
- All terminal availability, prices and booking terms are linked to
  Heathrow's live page.

### Lounges
- Official Heathrow lounges and hotels:
  https://www.heathrow.com/at-the-airport/lounges-hotels-spas
- Departure lounge and boarding guide:
  https://www.heathrow.com/departures/departure-lounge-and-boarding
- Heathrow states that lounges are available in all terminals and some
  can be booked in advance. The guide gives T2 examples (Lufthansa
  Senator/Business, Singapore Airlines SilverKris, Air Canada Maple Leaf,
  United Club and United Arrivals Lounge), distinguishing departures
  and arrivals lounges. It avoids treating all lounge access as free
  or universally available.

## Madrid — MAD

### Parking
- Official Aena T1 parking, maps and booking:
  https://www.aena.es/en/t1.html
- Official T4 parking:
  https://www.aena.es/en/adolfo-suarez-madrid-barajas/parking/t4.html
- Official T2 departures express car park:
  https://www.aena.es/en/madrid-barajas-adolfo-suarez/parking/t2/expres-salidas-t2.html
- Aena lists T1 P1, long-stay T1/T2/T3, low-cost, T4 P4, T4 First Class,
  T4 long-stay and T2 express products. T2 express allows ten minutes
  free and has a two-hour maximum; after ten minutes the charge is
  calculated from the start of the stay. The guide links to Aena for
  current rates, booking and terminal-specific access.

### Lounges
- Official Aena lounge directory:
  https://www.aena.es/en/adolfo-suarez-madrid-barajas/vip-services/vip-lounges/vip-lounges-all-terminals.html
- Official Aena VIP booking:
  https://serviciosvip.aena.es/vip/en/aeropuertos/adolfo-suarez-madrid-barajas
- Eight lounges are listed: Altitude and Cibeles (T1), Puerta de Alcalá
  (T2), Puerta del Sol (T3), Plaza Mayor and Retiro (T4), and Gran Vía
  and Neptuno (T4S). Prices and availability are delegated to Aena's
  live booking system.

## Publication rules and QA

  live booking/rate page; exceptions are not needed for this batch.
- Current closures and operational constraints are called out, notably
  Heathrow T4 parking and T2/T3 Priority Park & Ride.

  the operator page and April 2026 airport FAQ evidence conflict.
- Lounge products, hotels, relaxation spaces and public waiting rooms
  are distinguished rather than grouped under a false generic access rule.
- Python tests verify the 12 sections, source URLs, review metadata and
  the remaining two legitimate drafts per airport.
