## Munich Airport — MUC

### Parking
- Official parking catalogue:
  https://www.munich-airport.com/parking-260363
- Terminal parking:
  https://www.munich-airport.com/terminal-parking-261312
- Free transfer from selected Economy car parks:
  https://www.munich-airport.com/public-transport-260822
- Short-stop rules:
  https://www.munich-airport.com/meeters-and-greeters-27003122
- Accessible parking:
  https://www.munich-airport.com/accessible-travel-260945

The airport lists Terminal, Economy, Premium, Valet and special parking.
P1/P2/P3/P7 are convenient for T1, P8 is central between T1/T2, and
P20/P26 are positioned for T2. Economy parking includes P41/P43/P44/P50/
P51/P90/P91/P92, with selected parking tickets granting free S-Bahn or
bus transfers.

The official parking page states that **2 November 2026 through
approximately October 2027** bridge maintenance in Terminalstraße West
and Mitte causes traffic/pedestrian restrictions. The guide warns
travellers to follow on-site signs and allow extra time. Accessible
parking is available in all garages near entrances/elevators; these
spaces cannot be reserved.

### Lounges
- Official lounge directory:
  https://www.munich-airport.com/lounges-830529
- Lounge access/terminal transfer FAQ:
  https://www.munich-airport.com/frequently-asked-questions-376207
- Lufthansa lounge locations:
  https://www.munich-airport.com/lh-lounge-521172
- Airport overview of common-use lounges and VIP Wing:
  https://www.munich-airport.com/ten-reasons

The guide distinguishes Airport Lounge Europe (T1, Module D, Level 5)
from Airport Lounge World (T1, Module A, Level 4). Lounge World is in
the non-Schengen departure area and is limited to passengers on
non-Schengen flights, including eligible transit passengers. Lufthansa
lounges are in T2, gates G–L, levels 4/5; Emirates, Air France/KLM and
British Airways are among the T1 lounge listings. VipWing is described
as a separate VIP product, not a common-use lounge.

## Minsk National Airport — MSQ

### Parking
- Official parking page (shown as updated 10 November 2026):
  https://minskairport.com/parking/

The airport lists P1, P2, P3 and a separate passenger pick-up/drop-off
area. P1 has 1,235 spaces; P2 has 138 spaces. The P3 capacity is
inconsistent within the same official page (1,579 in the summary and
1,283 in the detailed description), so the guide deliberately does not
publish a P3 capacity number.

The current published tariff table is transcribed with a review-date
caveat: P1 6 BYN for the first four hours / 12 BYN per day; P2 2 BYN
for the first hour, 48 BYN per day, 336 BYN per week and 1,440 BYN
per month; P3 5 BYN per four hours / 10 BYN per day. P1 reservation
is available through the official service. Tariffs and free capacity
must be checked on the airport page/on-site signage before arrival.

### Lounges
- Official airport VIP/CIP and Business Lounge page:
  https://minskairport.com/vip/

The airport distinguishes:
- Business lounges on the fourth floor of the departure area in both
  international and Union sectors. Priority passengers may be invited;
  other passengers may pay. Standard stay is up to three hours.
- VIP/CIP lounge in the left wing, floors 1–3, open 24/7. It offers
  individual check-in and baggage handling, assistance through airport
  procedures, personal escort/transfer, meal, Wi-Fi and a conference
  room. Cash applications are requested one day before departure and
  non-cash applications three days before departure.

The guide does not freeze service tariffs; it directs passengers to
the airport's listed passenger-services contacts for current prices.

## Beijing Capital International Airport — PEK

### Parking
- Official BCIA parking building and rates page:
  https://www.bcia.com.cn/tcc.html

The official Chinese-language airport page identifies:
- T2 Parking Building: six floors and approximately 4,300 spaces;
  F1/F2 timed parking, B1 ride-hailing, B2/B3 long-term parking and
  B4 staff parking.
- T3 Parking Building: approximately 4,300 spaces; B1 passenger
  parking zones A/B/C, with B/C timed parking, A/E long-term parking
  and D for ride-hailing.

The current official page lists timed rates of CNY 3 per 10 minutes
for small cars and CNY 6 for larger vehicles, with under 10 minutes
free. Long-term rates differ by building and vehicle class. The guide
does not treat the static rate table as a guaranteed quote; airport
signage and the current official parking page control. Accessible
parking reservation is listed among the official special-passenger
services.

### Lounges
- Official BCIA services directory:
  https://www.bcia.com.cn/ssjfw.html
- Beijing Government 12345 official reply about hourly rest lounges:
  https://english.beijing.gov.cn/12345hotline/faqs/all/202506/t20250619_4117030.html
- Cathay Pacific official lounge page:
  https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_CN/destinations/lounges/beijing-pek/cathay-pacific-lounge.html
- Asiana Airlines official airport guide:
  https://flyasiana.com/C/SG/EN/travel/airport?airportCd=PEK

The guide distinguishes hourly rest lounges from airline/VIP lounges.
The Beijing Government's 29 May 2025 reply identifies the T2
international hourly lounge as operated by Ji Hotel and open 24 hours,
and identifies the T3 international hourly lounge in Area E. Cathay
Pacific lists its T3 lounge on Level 3 near Gate E20 with flight-linked
opening hours. Asiana's airport guide places the Air China Business
Lounge in the T2 international departure duty-free area after security
and immigration; its hours follow flight operations.

Lounge access, opening hours and terminal restrictions are not
generalized to all passengers; the guide tells users to confirm their
airline, terminal and current access rules before entering.
