## Munich Airport — MUC

### Parking

Official sources:
- Parking and booking: https://www.munich-airport.com/parking-260363
- Terminal parking and P8: https://www.munich-airport.com/terminal-parking-261312
- Economy parking options: https://www.munich-airport.com/economy-parking-261282
- Free public transport from selected Economy car parks:
  https://www.munich-airport.com/public-transport-260822
- Curbside short-stop rules:
  https://www.munich-airport.com/meeters-and-greeters-27003122
- Accessible parking:
  https://www.munich-airport.com/accessible-travel-260945

The current official pages confirm P1/P2/P3/P7 as convenient for T1, P8
between T1 and T2, and P20/P26 near T2. The Economy offer includes P41, P42,
P43, P44, P48, P50, P51 and P90; the public-transport page also identifies
P91/P92 among the airport parking areas. Availability, height limits and
booking restrictions are area-specific and must be checked before arrival.

The parking ticket grants free S-Bahn travel on S1/S8 between Besucherpark
and the terminals for the listed Economy areas. Bus 635 connects P41/P43 to
Terminalstraße Mitte; the benefit is not generalized to every car park.

The new P8 opened on **1 September 2026** between the terminals. The official
parking page currently advertises online-booking promotions, but no temporary
discount or live tariff is hardcoded into the passenger guide.

From **2 November 2026 until approximately October 2027**, maintenance on the
northern bridges around Terminalstraße West and Mitte causes traffic and
pedestrian restrictions. The guide retains this active warning.

Curbside lanes allow 10 minutes free on the first two entries per calendar
day; from the third entry, the free period no longer applies. Accessible
spaces are available in garages near entrances/elevators and cannot be
reserved. The official page specifically says P51 is not barrier-free.
Eligible disability-card holders can receive a 50% standard-fee reduction
through the parking office; it does not apply to online bookings or certain
special parking categories.

### Lounges

Official sources:
- Lounge directory: https://www.munich-airport.com/lounges-830529
- Airport Lounge Europe: https://www.munich-airport.com/airport-lounge-europe-725693
- Airport Lounge World: https://www.munich-airport.com/airport-lounge-world-35487554
- Access and terminal-change FAQ:
  https://www.munich-airport.com/frequently-asked-questions-376207
- Lufthansa lounges: https://www.munich-airport.com/lh-lounge-521172

The Airport Lounge Europe is in T1, Module D, Level 5 and is for Schengen
departures. Airport Lounge World is in T1, Gate A, Level 4, in the
non-Schengen departure area; only eligible non-Schengen passengers, including
qualifying transit passengers, can enter. The airport warns that Europe may
close temporarily when capacity is constrained. Opening hours and entry
prices are dynamic and are therefore linked to the live official pages rather
than frozen in the JSON.

Lufthansa lounges are in T2, Gates G–L, Levels 4 and 5, behind security.
Access remains subject to the passenger's ticket, status and Star Alliance
rules. The guide distinguishes VipWing as a separate VIP service.

## Minsk National Airport — MSQ

### Parking

Official source:
- Parking, current tariffs and accessibility rules:
  https://minskairport.com/parking/

The official parking page was dated **10 November 2026** when rechecked.
P1 has 1,235 spaces and P2 has 138. The same page lists P3 as 1,579 spaces
in its tariff summary and 1,283 in the detailed description; the guide
deliberately omits a P3 capacity number instead of choosing one arbitrarily.

The published tariffs remain:
- P1: BYN 6 for the first four hours; BYN 12 per day.
- P2: BYN 2 for the first hour; BYN 48 per day, BYN 336 per week and
  BYN 1,440 per month.
- P3: BYN 5 per four hours; BYN 10 per day.
- Controlled pick-up/drop-off area: BYN 10 per hour, BYN 240 per day,
  BYN 1,680 per week and BYN 7,200 per month.

The current page also states that, from **4 September 2026**, parking fees
are waived (excluding taxis) for vehicles driven by or transporting people
in the specified disability categories: groups I and II, and group III with
musculoskeletal disorders. The required disability-status document and
flight ticket must be presented. This newly explicit exemption has been
added to the guide.

P1 reservations are available through the airport's linked reservation
service. The airport asks drivers to check live capacity and signage; the
24-hour parking contact is +375 33 300 10 19. Tariffs remain subject to
the rate in force on the payment date.

### Lounges

Official source:
- Business Lounge and VIP/CIP:
  https://minskairport.com/vip/

The official page displayed a **15 November 2026** date at re-check. It
confirms Business Lounges on the fourth floor of the departure area in both
the international and Union sectors. Eligible priority passengers may be
invited; other passengers may pay. Business Lounge stays are limited to
three hours.

The airport distinguishes this from VIP/CIP service in the left wing,
floors 1–3, open 24/7. VIP/CIP stays are also limited to three hours, with
extra hours charged separately. Cash applications are requested one day
before departure; non-cash applications three days before departure.
A Business Class passenger invited to the Business Lounge should not assume
that VIP/CIP service is included; the airport describes VIP/CIP as a
separately paid service. Current rates should be confirmed with the airport.

## Beijing Capital International Airport — PEK

### Parking

Official source:
- BCIA parking buildings, zone layout and rates:
  https://www.bcia.com.cn/tcc.html

The current official page identifies a six-level T2 parking building with
approximately 4,300 spaces. Its current zone layout is F1/F2 for timed
parking, B1 for ride-hailing, B2/B3 for long-term parking and B4 for staff.
The airport notes that construction/upgrade work and fee-system changes may
alter on-site procedures; staff instructions and signs take precedence.

T3 also has approximately 4,300 spaces. Its currently open B1 passenger
parking zones are **A/B/C/E**: B/C are timed parking, A/E are long-term
parking, and D is the ride-hailing area. The previous draft omitted E from
the passenger-parking zones; this has been corrected.

The official timed tariff is CNY 3 per 10 minutes for small cars and CNY 6
for larger vehicles; stays under 10 minutes are free. Long-term rates and
eligibility depend on the building, vehicle class and designated zone.
Long-term parking is defined on the official page as continuous parking
over 5.5 hours in the marked long-term areas. Accessible-space reservations
are listed for special-needs passengers: T2 +86 10 64595535 and T3
+86 10 64535873. The guide directs passengers to the official page and
on-site signs rather than treating static rates as guaranteed quotes.

### Lounges

Official/operator sources:
- BCIA services directory: https://www.bcia.com.cn/ssjfw.html
- Beijing 12345 official response (29 May 2025):
  https://english.beijing.gov.cn/12345hotline/faqs/all/202506/t20250619_4117030.html
- Cathay Pacific lounge:
  https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_CN/destinations/lounges/beijing-pek/cathay-pacific-lounge.html
- Asiana Airlines PEK airport guide:
  https://flyasiana.com/C/SG/EN/travel/airport?airportCd=PEK

The Beijing 12345 response identifies the T2 international hourly-rest lounge
as operated by Ji Hotel and open 24 hours, and places the T3 international
hourly-rest lounge in Area E. Because this government response is dated
29 May 2025, the guide explicitly tells passengers to reconfirm operation
and phone numbers before relying on it.

Cathay Pacific currently lists its lounge in T3, Level 3, beside Gate E20,
with opening hours linked to its flight schedule. Asiana's PEK airport guide
lists the Air China Business Lounge in T2's international departure duty-free
area after security and immigration; its guide says hours follow the flight
schedule and that showers are unavailable during construction. Asiana's
guide also notes terminal relocation effective 1 September 2026, so T2/T3
transfers must be reconfirmed for the actual itinerary.

Hourly-rest lounges and airline lounges are distinct services; access,
terminal, opening hours and eligibility are not interchangeable.
